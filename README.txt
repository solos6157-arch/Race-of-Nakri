RACE OF NAKRI — PIXEL TYPING RACE
=================================

How to play:
1. Extract the ZIP.
2. Double-click index.html (Chrome, Edge, or Firefox).
3. Choose English or Khmer using the language button.
4. Press START RACE and type the shown words. English words are lowercase.
5. Complete 20 words before time runs out to win.
6. Win races to earn coins, then use the Garage to unlock cars.
7. Scores, coins, wins, and unlocked cars are saved in this browser.

Included:
- English / Khmer interface and word list
- Pixel-inspired retro racing visual style
- Six car tiers from starter to premium
- Typing race, WPM and accuracy tracking
- Coins/rewards and car shop
- Top 10 local leaderboard
- Your uploaded background song, converted from M4A to MP3 and set to loop (music toggle; starts after clicking because browsers block autoplay)
- Your supplied Mi Labuby Kdouy S’ouy picture used as the game profile/logo image
- Simple retro sound effects (toggle)
- Responsive desktop/mobile layout

Important:
This is a playable single-browser prototype. It supports repeated play and local
scores. A real online multiplayer race and shared global leaderboard require a
server/database and player accounts. The current Top 10 is stored on this
device/browser only. The included sound effects are generated retro tones;
a looping relaxing chiptune soundtrack can be added with a licensed/original
audio file later.

Music note: The uploaded audio was an M4A file, so it was converted to MP3 for this updated game. Background music volume starts at 28% and can be toggled on/off.
